## Notes

Open discovery used official Azure DocumentDB (learn.microsoft.com) pages and several installed skills that package best-practice guidance. I recorded 30 concise public-source practices most relevant to the sample app (vector indexing, embedding handling, connection and security, sharding, monitoring, projection, TTL and indexing budgets). Then each prior belief (K01..K24) was checked and mapped: most were confirmed by Azure DocumentDB docs or the installed skills. Two prior beliefs (K14 about writeConcern/readPreference and K20 about deterministic offline embeddings for tests) had no authoritative public guidance for Azure DocumentDB in the searches performed and were therefore recorded as prior_knowledge_only with the searches attempted listed in how_found.
