- **The model believed:** Use driver‑level options for retryable reads/writes where supported (retryReads/retryWrites) and be aware of differences in retry semantics between MongoDB drivers and Azure's MongoDB API; test retry behavior for your driver version. (knowledge of `mongodb`)
